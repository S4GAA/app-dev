# My Favorite Series

## Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)

**Money Heist** is my favorite television series. It is a Spanish crime and thriller series about a mysterious man called The Professor who plans a major heist with a group of people. Each member has a city name as their code name.

### Why I Like It
- The storyline is exciting and unpredictable.
- The characters are intelligent and interesting.
- The plot has many unexpected twists.
- It shows teamwork, friendship, and loyalty.
- Every episode makes me curious about what happens next.
